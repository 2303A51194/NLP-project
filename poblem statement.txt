Patients often face difficulties in evaluating hospital service quality due to the large volume of unstructured feedback and reviews.
Traditional methods of analyzing patient feedback are time-consuming and may not identify important service issues effectively.
There is a need for an NLP-based system to automatically analyze patient reviews and identify sentiments, complaints, and service-quality factors.
The system can analyze aspects such as doctor behavior, staff support, waiting time, cleanliness, facilities, and overall satisfaction in hospitals across Warangal.
This project aims to develop an NLP-based hospital service quality analysis system that provides actionable insights to improve patient experience and healthcare services.
