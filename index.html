NLP-Based Hospital Service Quality Analysis in Warangal

Natural Language Processing framework evaluating patient reviews across 12 Warangal hospitals to classify sentiment and isolate operational issues.

Official Project Abstract

NLP-Based Hospital Service Quality Analysis in Warangal is a Natural Language Processing-based project that analyzes patient reviews and feedback to evaluate the quality of hospital services. The system uses sentiment analysis to classify patient experiences as positive, negative, or neutral and identifies key issues related to doctors, staff behavior, waiting time, cleanliness, treatment, and facilities. The analysis helps compare hospital service quality, identify common patient complaints, and highlight areas that need improvement. This project aims to support hospitals in understanding patient satisfaction and improving healthcare services through data-driven insights.
